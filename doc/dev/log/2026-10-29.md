## RAM log, tuning, timed log, DRAM set points (branch `imx8m-dram-cleanup`)

- Merged `ramlog` (PR #70) into the branch; the RAM log moved to its own `.ramlog` section in
  the RAM pb runs from (on the i.MX8M `.no_init` is DRAM, trained after `ramlog_init()`). Enabled on
  jiffy and the imx8mpevk; console at warnings, RAM log at debug.
- EVK sweep of the DLL override taps in HS200: 124/128 pass, gaps at 14-15 and 78-79 (wrapping
  every 64 taps), a CMD21 is 15 us. The fixed tap matters (tap 0 failed reads). HS200 tuning now
  sweeps all taps (2 ms) and uses the middle of the widest run, falls back to HS 52 MHz. A coarse
  sweep missed the 2-tap gaps (picked 63 instead of 46).
- Found on the board: the auto CMD12 response is not in CMD_RSP3 yet when TC is set (stale CSD
  bits read as card errors): CMD12 is sent after the data now. The command wait was ~1 ms, too
  short for a CMD2 at 400 kHz: 10 ms.
- `ts()` removed, every LOG line starts with `[ms.us]` from `log_time_start()` (src/log.c).
  The firmware printf has no space padding, so no field width.
- `struct dram_rates`: the board selects the DRAM set points to train (a prefix of fsp_table,
  TF-A gets 0 for the rest). imx8mpevk: 4000 MT/s only, plat init 290 -> 75 ms; checked with
  4 MiB eMMC write/verify x3 and an SD read.
- EVK boot timeline (4000 only): DDR 0.6-65 ms, eMMC 75-123 ms (CMD1 ready ~17 ms, EXT_CSD at
  400 kHz ~24 ms), SD 123-172 ms (ACMD41 ~40 ms).
- The EVK eMMC now has a GPT: Jonas installed it (the default table, 13 partitions).
- Left: jiffy on the latest build (the ULINK needs a replug), DDR52/HS400 delay setup (F-113,
  F-116), UHS-I, raising the clock before the first EXT_CSD read (saves ~20 ms).

## CAAM review against the SRMs (same branch)

- Checked `imx_caam.c` against the i.MX6UL, 8M Mini and 8DXL/QXP SRMs (NDA, nothing quoted).
  Register names are now the SRM ones (`IRBAR_JR`, `ORSFR_JR`, `CAAMVID_MS`, `CHAVID_LS[MDVID]`...),
  plus field masks (ORSF is 10 bits, CAAMVID_MS IP_ID is 16 bits, it was masked to 12). The
  64-bit IRBAR/ORBAR writes go to +4: MCFGR[DWT] = 0 on a little endian SoC swaps the words.
  Dropped unused defines, among them SMCJR/SMCSJR at the wrong offsets (they're at 0xbe4/0xbec).
- Descriptors now use `desc_defines.h`: header, ALGORITHM OPERATION, and the ECDSA verify
  PROTOCOL OPERATION (PROTID 0x16, PROTINFO MES_REP = hashed, ECC) and PDB (PD, ECDSEL), new
  defines there. Macro arguments in `desc_defines.h` are now parenthesized. All 46 descriptor
  word variants were checked equal to the old expressions on the host, and the jiffy/imx8mpevk/
  imx8dxlevk objects are identical apart from that and the masks. Not run on a board.
- The SRMs disagree on the verify PDB's PD bit: the 8MM table puts it at bit 25, but its own SGF
  list uses bits 31-23 and the 6UL layout has a 9-bit SGF. pb uses bit 22, which works on jiffy.
  The 8MM table also says the Temp pointer is omitted for a public key verify; pb passes it and
  that works too. Left as is.
- The job ring at CAAM + 0x1000 is JR0, not JR1: `IMX6UL_CAAM_JR0_BASE`, `IMX8M_CAAM_JR0_BASE`.
  imx8x: `IMX_CAAM_JR2_BASE` (64 KiB pages) replaces three literal 0x31430000.
- New F-127 (job status from JRSTAR, not the output ring entry).
