## F-172 fixed: the cdns3 tried for SuperSpeed

- jiffy power cycled (Jonas); not used since.
- The cause: `cdns3_udc_core_init()` never set USB_CONF's USB3DIS, so after every init the
  controller tried for a SuperSpeed link, although pb's descriptors are USB 2.0 and it has only
  ever enumerated at high speed (the host's SuperSpeed hub ports never saw it). Linux's
  `cdns3_gadget_udc_start()` sets USB3DIS for a high speed gadget. When the host saw the device
  late, command mode's 10 s ready timeout reset the board, and the next init started the same
  race again: the 20 s to 2 min outages, and the writes that failed right after a reset.

  2 s between rounds: the old pb (`e880cd49`, BOOT1) 2 of 13 back within 2 s, the others 4.8 to
  150 s, the 13th lost; the new one (BOOT0, enabled) 30 of 30 in 0.9 to 1.3 s, no transport
  timeout on its console. Then 20 rounds of `dev reset` and a write as soon as `punchboot list`
  shows it (15 MiB, 4 KiB, 700 B in turns), each verified: 20 of 20.
- Not known: why the SuperSpeed attempt takes so long or fails with the VL813 (the hub's
  SuperSpeed side, the Salvo PHY's settings). It doesn't matter while pb is USB 2.0 only; a
  SuperSpeed pb would have to answer it.
- Left: the MEK's BOOT0 holds the fix, BOOT1 `e880cd49`'s. The usb.md conformance table no
