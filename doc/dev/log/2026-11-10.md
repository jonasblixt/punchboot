## The platform contract, Jonas's review

Jonas's six comments in `platform.md`, worked in and removed:

- "Don't boot silently when the TZC isn't operational, LOG_ERR is not enough": new 2.3, no silent
  boot. `plat_init()` records in `plat_get_status()` (3.8, which also takes over
  `plat_tz_dram_filter()`), `boot()` checks it against what the boot needs and fails into command
  mode. The rule is decided, the policy per condition is a proposal (F-248, open question).
- "Are there more things like this pb is responsible for? Check U-Boot": read U-Boot's
  `mx6/soc.c`, `init.c`, `imx8m/soc.c`, `jr.c` (mainline) and NXP's `secure_lockup()`, OP-TEE's
  plat-imx (`tzc380.c`, `imx_csu.c`, `imx_caam.c`, `conf.mk`), TF-A's i.MX8M BL31 setup, the 6UL RM
  (OCOTP 35.5.6, AIPS-TZ 14.6, WDOG 57.5.3). New: `plat_lockdown()` (4.4) and the other first-stage
  duties (4.5); F-244 (jiffy's CSF unlocks SRK revocation for pb and nothing locks it before the
  jump), F-245 (the WDOG power-down counters), F-246 (AIPS-TZ). Also learned: OP-TEE in its secure
  configuration panics when the 8M's TZASC isn't enabled and locked, or when region 0 isn't secure
  only. So pb opens DRAM in region 1 and leaves region 0 (F-208 updated); BL31 v2.12 opens region 0
  itself, which such an OP-TEE refuses.
- "Remove all NXP/i.MX specific references": `platform.md` is vendor neutral; the i.MX facts, the
  per-port tables and the conformance table are in the new `plat-imx.md`.
- "Why both BOARD_RAM and PB_RAM_LENGTH?": two memories, pb's own and the board's DRAM. Named
  apart now (`pb` region, `BOARD_DRAM_*`), and the `pb` length is derived from the ROM's load
  window, not set (F-239 updated). Also fixed: `buffers` is in OCRAM on the RT, not DRAM.
- "The RT's MEMORY move is an action": out of the contract, in F-239 and `plat-imx.md` 5.
- "Make an action of removing the 'not yet' lines when implemented": every marker names its
  finding (F-247 new for `plat_rot`/`plat_slc` and the crypto engine); `platform.md` says the fixing
  commit removes it and updates the conformance table; the checklist and `status.md` say so too.

Order for the code: the fixes (F-233, F-234, F-244, F-245, F-208, each a `bugfix:` commit), `main()`
